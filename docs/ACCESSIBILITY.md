# Accessibility Contract

MoonWitness can look grunge without behaving grunge.

## Non-negotiable

- All interactive controls expose a visible keyboard focus state.
- Minimum touch target: **44 × 44 CSS px**.
- Status is never communicated by color alone.
- Text embedded in generated concept images is never treated as accessible product text.
- Every graph has a semantic text/list alternative.
- Every correlation score has an explanation affordance.
- Every legal status distinguishes cited source law from MoonWitness analysis/review.
- Dialogs and drawers trap focus, close with Escape, and restore focus.
- Form errors are connected to their field and remain visible until resolved.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Auto-playing cinematic motion must not be required to understand a case.
- Decorative grain, scratches, noise, and overlays must not reduce content contrast.

## Writing accessibility

Use short sentences, everyday words first, technical term second.

Good:
- **Not enough yet.**
- **The trails are starting to line up.**
- **Still open.**
- **Cool. Now the law gets involved.**

Avoid forced slang, sarcasm around serious evidence, or mystery copy that hides what an action actually does.

## Evidence integrity

A visual state must never imply verification that the underlying record does not carry.

Examples:
- community submission ≠ verified evidence
- correlation ≠ causation
- legal analysis ≠ court judgment
- unresolved ≠ false
