Investigation semantics

From observation to inspectable record.

The visual system is designed as a chain of meaning. Capture evidence, express the relationship, verify provenance, then export without losing the audit trail.

01 · Observe

Evidence annotation

Bounding regions, timestamps, OCR, waveform cues, metadata, confidence, and review overlays.

 02 · Relate

Correlation semantics

Support, contradiction, reference, identity, temporal, legal, inferred, and unresolved relationships.

 03 · Verify

Integrity & custody

Hashes, signatures, provenance, custody transfer, tamper alerts, and immutable-record states.

 04 · Deliver

Export & seal

Verified stamps, QR citations, watermarks, signed export, and evidence-bundle delivery.
